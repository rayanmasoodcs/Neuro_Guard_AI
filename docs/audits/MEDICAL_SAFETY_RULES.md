# NeuroGuard AI — Medical Safety & Ethical AI Rules

**NeuroGuard AI** is an investigational AI healthcare and research prototype. Because this software operates in the domain of clinical neurology and seizure monitoring, all development, agent actions, UI text, and chatbot responses must adhere to these medical safety rules.

---

## MANDATORY MEDICAL SAFETY PRINCIPLES

### 1. No Definitive Diagnostic Claims
NeuroGuard AI is a clinical decision support and research exploration tool. The system and documentation must **never** claim to definitively diagnose epilepsy, seizure disorders, or any medical condition. All diagnostic interpretations belong exclusively to licensed medical professionals.

### 2. Explicit AI Output Identification
Every classification score, seizure event flag, anomaly alert, or timeline summary generated by machine learning models must be visibly labeled as **"AI Analysis"** or **"AI Prediction"**. Users and clinicians must never be misled into mistaking algorithmic inferences for validated human clinical reviews.

### 3. Absolute Prohibition on Chatbot Prescriptions
The patient chatbot, doctor assistant chatbot, and automated reasoning pipelines must **never** suggest, recommend, or generate new prescription medications. Any inquiry regarding drug therapy must direct the patient to their treating physician.

### 4. Absolute Prohibition on Dosage Modifications
Under no circumstances may the application, AI agent, or conversational interface suggest adjusting, pausing, escalating, or tapering any medication dosage. Medication schedules within the app are strictly for tracking patient compliance with existing doctor-prescribed regimens.

### 5. No False Certainty Regarding Emergency Outcomes
The platform must never offer unwarranted reassurance or absolute certainty regarding patient health status or safety outcomes during an active or suspected seizure event. Emergency protocols must always emphasize seeking immediate emergency medical care when in doubt.

### 6. Verification of Emergency Dispatch & Contact Status
The system must **never** state or imply that emergency services (such as 911/EMS or hospital teams) have been dispatched or contacted unless a verified technical integration has confirmed delivery and acknowledgment of the dispatch signal. If only local alarms or SMS triggers are dispatched, status messages must state this precisely.

### 7. Transparent Communication of Uncertainty and Limitations
When input signals have high artifact contamination, low signal-to-noise ratio, disconnected electrode leads, or out-of-distribution patterns, the application must explicitly display high uncertainty warnings and declare its analytical limitations rather than projecting false confidence.

### 8. No Uncertified Regulatory Claims
The system, documentation, marketing copy, and UI strings must **never** claim FDA clearance, CE mark certification, HIPAA certification, or any formal medical device regulatory approval unless official regulatory clearance documentation has been formally obtained and validated.
